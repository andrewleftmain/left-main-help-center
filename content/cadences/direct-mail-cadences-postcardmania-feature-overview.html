Feature Overview

Release Date: April 29, 2026

Direct Mail in Left Main is now fully integrated into the Cadence experience, allowing you to include physical mail as part of a unified, multi-channel outreach strategy.

Powered by PostcardMania, you can design and send branded mail pieces directly from Salesforce, then incorporate them into structured Cadences alongside your other outreach activities. This allows your team to manage all follow-up—from digital to physical touchpoints—within a single, coordinated workflow.

By bringing Direct Mail into Cadences, Left Main helps you stay consistent, reduce manual work, and scale your outreach more effectively.

Who is this for?

  • Real estate investment teams managing multi-channel outreach

  • Acquisition teams looking to improve follow-up consistency

  • Organizations that want to combine digital and physical touchpoints in one workflow

When should they use it?

  • When building multi-channel Cadences that include physical mail

  • When you want to stay top of mind with prospects using both digital and offline outreach

  • When scaling outreach and needing a single system to manage all touchpoints

Key Capabilities

  • Send Direct Mail as part of a unified Cadence workflow

  • Design and manage mail templates using the PostcardMania app within Salesforce

  • Combine Direct Mail with Email, SMS, and Tasks in a structured cadence

  • Schedule and automate mail delivery alongside other outreach steps

  • Execute outreach directly from Salesforce without switching tools

  • Track mail activity alongside other cadence touchpoints

When to Use It

Scenario 1: Multi-Channel Follow-Up Strategy
Build a Cadence that combines calls, texts, and postcards to create a consistent and coordinated outreach sequence.

Scenario 2: Long-Term Nurture
Use periodic Direct Mail within Cadences to stay in front of leads over time without relying only on digital communication.

Scenario 3: Re-Engaging Cold Leads
Add Direct Mail to reach prospects who are no longer responding to calls or messages.

Scenario 4: Scaling Outreach Across Teams
Standardize outreach by using Cadence templates that include Direct Mail, allowing your team to execute campaigns consistently at scale.
