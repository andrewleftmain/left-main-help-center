Overview

This article explains how to install Postcard Mania

Steps

  1. From your Salesforce org home page, click the Waffle (app launcher) icon in the top left corner.

  2. At the bottom of the dropdown, click View All.

  3. In the modal that opens, click Visit AppExchange.

  4. In the AppExchange search bar, type Postcard Mania.

  5. Click the PCM Integrations app from the search results.

  6. Click Get It Now.

  7. Log into your Salesforce account using your credentials and grant the requested permissions.

  8. Check your email for the single-use verification code sent by Postcard Mania or Salesforce.

  9. Enter the verification code and submit.

  10. Review and agree to the terms and conditions, then click Install.

  11. Log into Salesforce once more when prompted.

  12. On the installation options screen, select Install for All Users.

    1. Click Install.

  13. When the popup appears asking you to approve third-party access, click Grant Access and continue.

    1. Installation may take a few minutes. Salesforce may prompt you to continue working and notify you by email when installation is complete.

  14. Once installation is finished, you will receive a confirmation email from Salesforce.
