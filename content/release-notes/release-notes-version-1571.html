Release Notes - DealSignals

This release delivers powerful usability improvements, smarter controls, and more flexible configuration options—giving your team better visibility, cleaner data, and greater control over how DealSignals works for your workflow.

Clearer Property Insights at a Glance

Last Change Summary & Related Property Information
We’ve enhanced property visibility by surfacing the most recent changes directly in the experience. You can now quickly understand what changed and why it matters—without digging through historical records—making it easier to prioritize action and stay informed.

Flexible Property Snoozing

Snooze with Duration & Auto-Unsnooze
You now have more control over how properties flow through your pipeline. Temporarily snooze properties for a defined period up to 45 days, with automatic reactivation when the snooze expires. This helps reduce noise while ensuring important opportunities never fall through the cracks.

Smarter Phone Number Tracking & Dispositioning

Phone Number Status Tagging in the Property Component

We’ve enhanced the phone number experience within the property Lightning Web Component to give your team better visibility into which numbers actually work.

Each phone number can now be tagged using simple drop-down controls—such as Valid, Disconnected, Invalid, or Wrong Number—making it easy to track call outcomes after skip tracing. These statuses are clearly surfaced in the component, helping teams avoid repeated outreach to bad numbers and focus efforts on contacts that convert.

This improvement supports cleaner data, more efficient calling workflows, and better collaboration across teams working the same property

Greater Control with Advanced Settings & Notifications

Notifications, Advanced Settings, and DealSignals Setup
This release introduces enhanced configuration options, giving you more flexibility in how DealSignals is set up and managed. Fine-tune notifications, control advanced settings, and tailor the platform to align with your team’s operational needs—all from within the UI.

These enhancements are designed to improve efficiency, data confidence, and day-to-day usability—so your team can focus on acting on the right opportunities at the right time.
