DispoSignals Release Notes

Summary

This release introduces DispoSignals, a solution designed to help real estate investors identify buyers, evaluate pricing, and analyze market activity directly within their CRM.

Availability & Access

  • Available as a paid add-on (included in Enterprise Edition)

  • Users without access will not see DispoSignals components in their CRM

Where to Find DispoSignals

Once enabled, users can access DispoSignals as a new tab and on the following objects:

  • Opportunities

  • Transactions

Additionally, DispoSignals can be added to the Activity component.

New Capabilities

  • Generate targeted buyer lists with SkipTrace data

  • Analyze investor activity and pricing trends

  • Compare investor, retail, and CRM comps within a single workflow

Usage Thresholds

DispoSignals usage includes the following monthly thresholds (additional credits available at additional cost):

  • Investor Activity: 100 runs

  • Investor Comps: 100 runs

  • Retail Comps: 100 runs

  • CRM Comps: Unlimited

  • Buyer Search: 20 runs
