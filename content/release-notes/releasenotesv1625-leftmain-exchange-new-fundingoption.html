Release Notes v1.625: Left Main Exchange New Funding Option

Release Date: September 18, 2026

Summary

Funding Options adds a new financing provider: Coast 2 Coast Capital, a medium-term wholetail funding option.

New Features

Coast 2 Coast Capital

Coast 2 Coast Capital funds up to 100% of the purchase price on wholetail deals — a medium-term option (90 or 180 days) for deals that need more time than a same-day close.

Terms:

  • Loan amount: $75,000 minimum

  • Max ARV: $800,000

  • Max LTAIV: 80%

  • Property condition: cosmetic repairs only

  • 90-day term: 11% + 2 points

  • 180-day term: 12% + 2 points

From the Coast 2 Coast option in Left Main REI, you can:

  1. Send relevant deal information directly from the Transaction — the request opens pre-filled with your property, deal, and closing details.

  2. Coast 2 Coast reviews and underwrites the deal.

  3. Coast 2 Coast issues terms — a 90-day or 180-day term, each with pricing shown up front.

  4. Funds at close, covering up to 100% of the purchase price.

Availability depends on property state; Coast 2 Coast confirms eligibility for each deal.

Which option should I use? Need a medium-term wholetail funding option (90–180 days)? Start with Coast 2 Coast Capital.
