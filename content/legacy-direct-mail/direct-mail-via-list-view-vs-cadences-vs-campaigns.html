Using Left Main’s Direct Mail app provides the opportunity to centralize and track all Direct Mail data in one place. This article outlines various methods to submit Direct Mail orders and considerations for each.

Direct Mail Feature Compatibility

Send Direct Mail From List View

Considerations

  • Bulk selection is limited to approximately 100-200 records depending on the number of background processes running in an org.

  • Using the Campaigns object to send Direct Mail in bulk is recommended for improved tracking and reporting capabilities.

Apply a Direct Mail Cadence From the Lead or Opportunity Record

Considerations

  • Direct Mail Cadences are only supported on the Lead and Opportunity objects

  • Applying Direct Mail Cadences in bulk is not supported by default, as using the Campaigns object to send Direct Mail in bulk is recommended for improved tracking and reporting capabilities.

Send Direct Mail From the Campaign List View

Considerations

  • Selecting more than one campaign from Campaign list view to begin a Direct Mail order is not yet supported.

  • Recommendation: Plan the budget and expected response rate for Direct Mail campaigns annually using Campaign hierarchy. Analyze and adjust Campaign success on a quarterly and monthly basis to inform planning for the following fiscal year.

    • Example

      • Parent Campaign: FY25 Marketing

      • Child Campaign: 2025 Direct Mail

      • Grandchild Campaign: 2025 FQ1 Direct Mail: Pre-Foreclosures
