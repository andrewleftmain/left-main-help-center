TWNSQR Integration Deprecated

The Left Main - TWNSQR integration has been deprecated and is no longer functional. Thus, it is safe to remove TWNSQR components from your Left Main pages. This step ensures that your visual layout remains uncluttered and that you are not relying on obsolete functionalities.

How to Remove Deprecated TWNSQR Buttons from Salesforce List Views

Managing list views in Salesforce is essential for administrators aiming to maintain a clean and efficient user interface. This article will guide you through the process of removing deprecated TWNSQR buttons from the Leads, Opportunities, and Transactions objects in Salesforce. It is crucial to ensure that your organization does not rely on outdated components, as their removal enhances visual clarity and functionality.

To successfully remove the deprecated TWNSQR buttons from the Leads, Opportunities, and Transactions objects, please follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your Left Main account.

  2. Navigate to the Setup section. This can usually be found in the upper right corner of the screen.

  3. In the Quick Find box, type Object Manager and select it.

  4. Choose the object (Leads, Opportunities, or Transactions) from which you want to remove the TWNSQR buttons.

  5. Once on the object page, select List View Button Layout.

  6. Under Custom Buttons, locate the TWNSQR button in the Selected Button section.

  7. Highlight the Twnsqr button and select Remove arrow.

  8. Click Save.

Please do the following for all three objects: Leads, Opportunities, Transactions.

By following these steps, you can efficiently remove any unnecessary TWNSQR buttons and streamline your Lightning page layouts. This will enhance the user experience for your team members.
