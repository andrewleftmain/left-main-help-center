Feature Overview: Investor Portal

Overview

Left Main Exchange (LMX): Investor Portal is a fully branded investor experience that allows organizations to market investment opportunities, engage buyers, collect offers, and manage transactions while keeping Salesforce as the system of record.

The solution combines four core components:

  1. Investor Portal

  2. Portal Setup

  3. Left Main Synchronization

  4. Portal Admin Dashboard

Together, these provide a complete investor acquisition and transaction management platform directly connected to Left Main REI.

Who is this for?

Administrators

Configure branding, domains, integrations, permissions, and portal settings.

Disposition Teams

Publish listings, monitor investor engagement, manage offers, and communicate with buyers.

Transaction Coordinators

Share documents, exchange notes, and track active transactions.

Investors

Browse opportunities, save properties, submit offers, and manage active transactions.

When Should You Use It?

Use LMX Investor Portal when you want to:

  • Market properties to investors

  • Centralize buyer communications

  • Track investor engagement

  • Collect and manage offers

  • Share transaction documents

  • Provide investors with a self-service experience

  • Keep Left Main and investor activity synchronized

Core Components

Investor Portal

The investor-facing website where investors browse listings, submit offers, save properties, and manage active transactions.

Portal Setup

The Salesforce configuration center used to launch and maintain the portal.

Left Main Synchronization

The synchronization engine that connects Salesforce and the Investor Portal.

Portal Admin Dashboard

The operational dashboard used to monitor investor activity and portal performance.
