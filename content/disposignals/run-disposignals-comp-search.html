How to Run a DispoSignals Comp Search

Types of Comparable Sale Searches

CRM Comps

CRM Comps use your own historical deal data inside Left Main CRM.

Use CRM Comps to:

  • Reference previously closed won deals

  • Analyze internal pricing trends

  • Validate assumptions using real company outcomes

CRM Comps are best used for:

  • Internal benchmarking

  • Historical pricing validation

  • Reviewing prior team performance

Retail Comps

Retail Comps provide traditional retail comparable sales data.

Use Retail Comps to:

  • Evaluate end-buyer pricing

  • Estimate retail market value

  • Compare investor vs. retail exit opportunities

Retail Comps are best used when evaluating:

  • ARV assumptions

  • Retail disposition strategies

  • Market positioning

Investor Comps

Investor Comps focus on comparable transactions from active investors.

Use Investor Comps to:

  • Understand what investors are paying for similar properties

  • Review acquisition behavior and pricing trends

  • Compare against current investor activity in the market

Investor Comps are best used when evaluating:

  • Wholesale opportunities

  • Investor-focused exits

  • Acquisition pricing
