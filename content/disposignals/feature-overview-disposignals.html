Feature Overview: DispoSignals

Overview

DispoSignals is a data-driven solution that helps real estate investors evaluate pricing, identify buyers, and analyze market demand all from a single property address. By combining targeted buyer generation, investor activity insights, investor summary, and investor, retail, and CRM comps, DispoSignals enables faster, more informed acquisition and disposition decisions directly within the CRM. As you work with DispoSignals, you can quickly reference property details using the Subject Property snapshot, explore results in List view or Map View where supported, and sort results to better analyze data.

Who is this for?

  • Disposition managers prospecting new buyers

  • Acquisitions teams evaluating exit strategies and negotiating contracts

  • Teams looking to improve pricing visibility, buyer targeting, and deal velocity

When should you use it?

  • When preparing to disposition a property

  • When evaluating whether a deal is viable in a given market

  • When validating pricing against investor demand and comparable sales

  • When identifying and targeting the most relevant buyers

  • When comparing investor vs. retail exit opportunities

Key Capabilities

Investor Activity

Analyze buyer demand and pricing trends, and investor activity before executing outreach.

  • View buyer activity and demand trends without revealing identities

  • Based on investor purchase price activity to better reflect what investors are actively paying in the market

  • Filter by:

    • Property type

    • Investor type (e.g., landlord, flipper)

    • Additional behavioral and investment criteria

Best used for:
Market research, opportunity vetting, and pricing validation before outreach or acquisition.

Investor Summary

Provides a consolidated view of investor activity around a subject property.

  • Summarize investor activity, flipper trends, landlord metrics, and acquisition insights

  • Quickly evaluate market demand before diving into detailed search results

  • Complements Investor Activity by providing a high-level market overview

Best used for:
Evaluating overall investor demand and market conditions before pricing or buyer outreach.

Investor Comps

Analyze comparable transactions from active investors.

  • Understand investor pricing, acquisition strategies, and behavior

  • Evaluate how similar properties are being purchased by investors

Best used for:
Evaluating investor-focused pricing and disposition strategies.

Retail Comps

View traditional retail comparable sales.

  • Evaluate end-buyer pricing and exit potential

  • Compare against investor comps for strategy alignment

Best used for:
Comparing investor and retail exit opportunities.

CRM Comps

Access comparable transactions from your own historical deal data.

  • Pull comps from previously closed deals within your CRM

  • Gain visibility into your team’s actual performance and pricing trends

  • Provides a reliable baseline based on real internal outcomes

  • Helps validate deal assumptions using your own transaction history

Best used for:
Validating pricing decisions using data your company already owns.

Full Property Analysis

Run multiple DispoSignals analyses from a single action.

  • Simultaneously runs Investor Activity, Investor Summary, Investor Comps, and Retail Comps

  • Streamlines property evaluation by reducing the number of individual searches

  • Provides a more complete view of market activity and pricing in one workflow

Best used for:
Performing a comprehensive property evaluation before making acquisition or disposition decisions.

Buyer Search

Generate up to 30 targeted buyers directly from a single property address.

  • Includes SkipTrace contact information and buyer names

  • Buyers derived from relevant transaction history and investment activity

  • Filter by:

    • Property type

    • Investor type (e.g., landlord, flipper)

    • Additional behavioral and investment criteria

Additional capabilities:

  • Create Buyer Contacts and and Interested Buyers directly from results

  • Search your own database to identify buyers from previous deals

Best used for:
Disposition execution and buyer outreach workflows.

When to Use It

Market Research & Opportunity Vetting

Use Investor Activity to understand demand, activity, and buyer presence before going under contract.

Deal Evaluation

Use Full Property Analysis to combine Investor Summary, Investor Activity, Retail and Investor Comps or run those separately to evaluate market demand, determine pricing strategy and identify the best exit strategy.

Pipeline Prioritization

Identify which properties have the strongest buyer demand and should be prioritized for disposition efforts.

Dispositions (Execution)

Use Buyer Search to generate targeted outreach lists with contact details and quickly connect with likely buyers once a property is under contract on the Transactions object.

Pro Tip

Start with Full Property Analysis on the Opportunity record to understand buyer demand, market activity and pricing trends. Then use Buyer Search on high-potential properties from the Transaction record to generate targeted outreach lists and move deals faster.

Key Takeaway

DispoSignals centralizes buyer intelligence, pricing visibility, and comparable sales into a single workflow inside Left Main REI, helping acquisitions and dispositions teams make more informed decisions using the same data.
