How to Run a DispoSignals Investor Activity Search

Overview

Investor Activity helps you analyze buyer demand, pricing trends, and investor behavior before executing outreach or committing to a deal.

By reviewing real investor purchase activity, you can better understand:

  • What investors are actively paying in a market

  • Areas with strong buyer demand

  • Pricing trends for similar properties

  • Whether a deal aligns with current investor activity

Unlike Buyer Search, Investor Activity is designed for research and pricing analysis rather than direct outreach.
