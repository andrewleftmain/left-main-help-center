DealSignals Feature Overview

Overview

DealSignals is an intelligent lead enrichment and prioritization solution that continuously monitors property records for new signs of seller motivation. Instead of relying solely on inbound leads or purchased lists, DealSignals identifies life events and property changes that indicate someone may be more likely to sell, then enriches existing leads and opportunities or creates new leads, and provides skip-traced contact information for new leads so your team can begin outreach faster. DealSignals helps investors focus on the people who are most likely to convert.

Who is DealSignals For?

DealSignals is designed for real estate investment teams that want to prioritize outreach based on seller motivation rather than working static lead lists.

It is especially valuable for:

  • Acquisition Managers

  • Inside Sales Agents

  • Lead Managers

  • Disposition Teams

  • Operations Managers

  • Team Leaders looking to improve follow-up efficiency

Whether you are managing hundreds or thousands of records, DealSignals helps your team spend time on the opportunities most likely to result in a deal.

Why Does DealSignals Matter?

Most CRM databases become less valuable over time as leads go cold or sellers are not ready when first contacted.

DealSignals does not just tell you who may be ready to sell. It helps you reach them faster. By combining real-time seller signals with available skip-traced contact information for new leads, your team can spend less time researching prospects and more time having meaningful conversations with property owners.

Instead of asking:

"Who should we call today?"

DealSignals answers:

"Who became motivated to sell today?"

This allows teams to:

  • Prioritize high-value opportunities

  • Re-engage dormant leads at the right time

  • Reduce wasted outreach

  • Improve speed-to-lead

  • Maximize the value of existing CRM data

When Should You Use DealSignals?

When Existing Leads or Opportunities Become More Relevant

Use DealSignals to identify existing records that may deserve renewed attention after a new property event or stress indicator is detected.

Example: An owner said they were not interested eight months ago. A new first up (death notice) and probate filing are later detected. DealSignals enriches the existing record and surfaces it on the Hot List, allowing your team to reconnect when the owner's circumstances may have changed.

When You Need to Prioritize a Large Database

Use DealSignals when your CRM contains more records than your team can realistically contact at once. Intent Scores and Hot Lists help users focus on the properties showing the strongest signs of seller motivation.

Example: Your CRM contains 20,000 leads. Rather than working through the list alphabetically, your team begins with the highest-priority records based on Intent Score and newly detected events.

When You Need to Keep Daily Outreach Organized

Use the Contacted and Snooze actions to keep active lists focused and prevent duplicate or unnecessary outreach.

Example: An agent contacts a property owner who is not ready to sell. The agent marks the record as Contacted and selects Snooze, temporarily removing it from the active queue and creating a follow-up task. If a new stress event occurs before then, the record resurfaces automatically.

When a Qualifying Property Owner Is Not Already in Your CRM

DealSignals may create a new Prospect Lead when a qualifying property does not match an existing Lead or Opportunity.

Example: DealSignals identifies a property with seller-motivation signals that is not already represented in your CRM. It creates a new Lead with available skip-traced contact information, allowing your team to begin calling, texting, emailing, or adding the prospect to an approved outreach process without first researching the owner manually.

Key Features

Real-Time Property Signals

DealSignals monitors properties for life events and distress indicators that often signal increased motivation to sell, including:

  • First Up (Death Notices)

  • Eviction

  • Pre-Foreclosure

  • Tax Default

  • Absentee Owner

  • Probate

  • Vacant

  • Default/Lis Pendens

  • Expired (Stale) Listing

  • Divorce

  • Utility Lien

These events can either enrich existing records or create new prospects automatically.

Skip Traced Contact Information

When DealSignals creates a new Prospect record, it automatically includes available skip-traced contact information, helping your team begin outreach without the need for a separate skip tracing service.

Depending on the available data, contact information may include:

  • Phone numbers

  • Email addresses

By providing contact information alongside property signals, DealSignals helps reduce manual research and allows acquisition teams to act on new opportunities more quickly.

Revive Existing Opportunities

Instead of letting older leads go stale, DealSignals automatically brings inactive leads and opportunities back into focus when new property activity occurs.

Benefits:

  • Re-engage previous prospects at the right time

  • Reduce missed opportunities

  • Maximize the value of your existing CRM data

Intent Scoring

Every property receives an Intent Score based on the number and significance of detected signals.

Teams can quickly prioritize:

  • High-intent sellers

  • Properties with multiple distress indicators

  • Opportunities most likely to convert

Daily Hot List

The Hot List automatically highlights the highest-priority properties each day.

Users can:

  • Review the highest intent scores first

  • Filter by geography, owner, property type, or signal

  • Begin outreach immediately

This creates a repeatable daily workflow focused on the best opportunities.

Contacted Tracking

A single click marks when outreach has been completed after enrichment.

This enables reporting on:

  • Speed to first contact

  • Follow-up performance

  • Contact rates

  • Conversion comparisons between contacted and untouched leads

Snooze

When a lead is not ready today, users can Snooze it.

Snoozing:

  • Removes the record from active Hot Lists

  • Creates a 30-day follow-up reminder

  • Automatically resurfaces the record if a new property signal occurs before the reminder

This keeps lists clean without losing visibility into future opportunities.

Smart Organization

DealSignals keeps enriched prospects separate from traditional inbound leads using:

  • Record Type: Prospect*

  • Lead Source: DealSignals*

*These are our recommendations. Record type and lead source can be modified to your company’s preference during setup.

This makes it easy to:

  • Build dedicated list views

  • Track adoption

  • Measure conversions

  • Report on DealSignals performance independently from other lead sources.

Notifications & Alerts

Teams can receive automated notifications for important events such as:

  • New stress indicators

  • Auction date updates

These alerts help teams respond faster when seller motivation changes.
